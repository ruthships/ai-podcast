Anthropic Overtakes OpenAI

A new TIME investigation reports Anthropic has overtaken OpenAI in annualized revenue and private valuation in recent months. OpenAI CEO Sam Altman admitted “some missteps,” and President Greg Brockman has taken over nearly all product and business operations.

Salesforce and Anthropic Launch “Claudeforce”

Salesforce announced Claudeforce, making Claude the default reasoning model across Agentforce, Slack, and a new sales-focused plugin with thirty-seven pre-built skills. Salesforce shares rallied more than thirty percent combined across two trading days on the earnings beat and the news together.

OpenAI’s Jalapeño Chip vs. Nvidia Blackwell

At Hot Chips 2026, OpenAI and Broadcom presented benchmarks for a new custom chip called Jalapeño, showing 1.5–1.9x better performance per watt and 1.7–3.6x lower latency than Nvidia’s Blackwell — though SemiAnalysis called the comparison “somewhat incomplete and unfair.”

Apple’s M6 and M5 Ultra

Apple unveiled the M6, its first chip built on a two nanometer process, along with the M5 Ultra. Apple says the M6 delivers about thirty percent higher AI graphics performance than the M5, and the M5 Ultra can run hundred-billion-parameter language models entirely on-device.

Toronto’s Veeda AI Launches with $90M+ Seed Round

Toronto-based Veeda AI launched with more than ninety million dollars in seed funding from Radical Ventures and Khosla Ventures. Founded by former Nvidia researchers led by CEO Sanja Fidler, the company builds “world models” that let robots learn physical tasks through simulated trial and error.

RAG Is Simpler Than You Think

A widely discussed piece compared six approaches to retrieval-augmented generation, or RAG, arguing that simpler architectures are often sufficient for most business use cases compared with the complex, heavily engineered pipelines that have become common.
