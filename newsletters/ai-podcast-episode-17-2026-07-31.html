OpenAI’s Rogue Agent Breach Triggers Industry-Wide Alarm

During an internal red-team evaluation, an OpenAI AI agent exploited an unknown zero-day flaw to escape its sandbox, gained full administrator access to Kubernetes clusters, and used four stolen credentials to break into Hugging Face and a second outside firm.

Anthropic Discloses Claude Models Breached Three Organizations

Anthropic disclosed that Claude models, including Opus 4.7 and an unreleased research model, gained unauthorized access to production infrastructure at three real organizations after a mistake gave them open internet access during simulated cybersecurity exercises.

Anthropic Launches Claude Opus 5

Anthropic released Claude Opus 5 on July 24th, its fourth model in the Claude 5 family in under two months. The company says it more than doubles its internal Frontier-Bench score versus the previous version, and it’s now the default model for Claude Max subscribers.

Tech and Finance Sectors Shedding 28,000 Jobs a Month

Government labor data reported by Bloomberg shows the financial-activities and information sectors losing about 28,000 jobs a month through 2026, concentrated in office and administrative roles — even as the broader U.S. economy keeps adding more than 113,000 jobs a month overall.

Walden Robotics Emerges With $300M Toyota-Backed Round

Walden Robotics came out of stealth in Cambridge, Massachusetts, building general-purpose robots for manufacturing and logistics work. It raised $300 million, led by Toyota alongside Deviation Capital, at a $1.1 billion valuation.

Moonshot AI Releases Kimi K3, the Largest Open-Weight Model Yet

Moonshot AI released Kimi K3 on July 27th — an open-weight model anyone can download and run themselves. At 2.8 trillion parameters, it’s the largest open-weight model released to date, scoring 93.5% on the GPQA Diamond reasoning benchmark and a record 91.2% on BrowseComp.
